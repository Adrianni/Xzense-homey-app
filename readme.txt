Control and monitor a chimney fan controller over Bluetooth LE, using the built-in Bluetooth of your Homey Pro. Change the fan speed, switch between boost (lighting) and ventilation, and follow the state, temperatures and chimney alarm in Homey and in your flows.

The controller accepts one Bluetooth connection at a time, so close other apps that talk to it. To pair, choose the controller from the list of nearby devices and enter its four-character PIN. This is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with the manufacturer.
